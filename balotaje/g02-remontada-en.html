Brazil's first runoff comeback

Margin of the first-round leader over the runner-up, in the first round and in the runoff. Latin American presidential runoffs since 1989.

Source: electoral authorities of each country (via Wikipedia) and TSE. Margins in points of valid votes. In Latin America, the runner-up turned the result around in 22 of 62 runoffs since 1990; in Brazil, never until 2026. *In Peru 2000 (Toledo) and Costa Rica 2014 (Araya), the runner-up abandoned the race before the runoff. In Haiti 2010 the first round is the preliminary count (Martelly made the runoff after an OAS review, with no new official percentages).
